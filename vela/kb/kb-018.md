title: USA tourist visa - required documents for Indian passport holders
country: USA
passport: IN
last_updated: 2026-08-01
---
Applicants holding an Indian passport must submit:
- Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the stay
- A recent photo that meets the US photo rules
- Proof of ties to India, such as employment or property papers
- A confirmed interview appointment

Processing time: depends on the interview date. After the interview, a decision usually takes 5 working days.
Fee: USD 185.
