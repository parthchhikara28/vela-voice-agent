title: Refund policy for service fees
country: All
passport: ANY
last_updated: 2026-08-01
---
The Vela Visas service fee is not refundable once an application has been submitted.
If a traveller cancels before submitting, the service fee is refunded in full within 7 working days.
Refund requests are handled by a human agent. The assistant must transfer the call and must not promise a refund.
