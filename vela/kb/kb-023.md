title: Australia visitor visa - required documents for Indian passport holders
country: Australia
passport: IN
last_updated: 2026-08-01
---
Applicants holding an Indian passport must submit:
- Passport valid for the whole stay, with 2 blank pages
- Bank statements for the last 6 months
- A day-by-day travel itinerary
- Proof of employment or business ownership

Processing time: 20 working days.
Fee: AUD 190 for a stay of up to 3 months.
