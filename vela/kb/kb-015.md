title: UAE tourist visa - required documents for Indian passport holders
country: UAE
passport: IN
last_updated: 2026-08-01
---
Applicants holding an Indian passport must submit:
- Passport valid for at least 6 months, with 2 blank pages
- A passport-size photo with a white background
- Confirmed return flight ticket
- Hotel booking for the full stay

Processing time: 4 working days.
Fee: AED 350 for a 30-day single-entry visa.
