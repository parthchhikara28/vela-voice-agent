title: Thailand tourist visa - required documents for Indian passport holders
country: Thailand
passport: IN
last_updated: 2026-08-01
---
Applicants holding an Indian passport must submit:
- Passport valid for at least 6 months, with 2 blank pages
- Bank statement for the last 3 months
- Hotel booking for the first 7 nights
- Confirmed return flight ticket

Processing time: 5 working days.
Fee: THB 2000 for a single-entry visa.
