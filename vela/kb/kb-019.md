title: Singapore visit visa - required documents for Indian passport holders
country: Singapore
passport: IN
last_updated: 2026-08-01
---
Applicants holding an Indian passport must submit:
- Passport valid for at least 6 months, with 2 blank pages
- A passport-size photo with a white background
- Confirmed return flight ticket
- Hotel booking for the full stay

Processing time: 3 working days.
Government fee: SGD 30.
Vela Visas service fee for Singapore: USD 20. This replaces the general service fee for Singapore applications.
