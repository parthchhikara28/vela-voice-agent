title: UK visitor visa - required documents for Indian passport holders
country: UK
passport: IN
last_updated: 2026-08-01

Applicants holding an Indian passport must submit:
- Passport valid for the whole stay, with 2 blank pages
- Bank statements for the last 6 months
- Proof of accommodation or an invitation letter
- Return flight booking

Processing time: 15 working days.
Fee: GBP 115 for a visit of up to 6 months.
