title: Schengen visa - required documents for Indian passport holders
country: Schengen
passport: IN
last_updated: 2026-08-01
---
Applicants holding an Indian passport must submit:
- Passport valid 3 months beyond intended departure, with 2 blank pages
- Travel medical insurance covering EUR 30,000
- Proof of accommodation for the full stay

Processing time: 15 calendar days (standard), 30 days if additional documents are requested.
Fee: EUR 90 adult, EUR 45 for children aged 6 to 12.
